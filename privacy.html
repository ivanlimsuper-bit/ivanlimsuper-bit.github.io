Privacy Policy - SSSuper Automation. Last updated 30 June 2026. 1. Introduction. This Privacy Policy explains how the SSSuper Automation application (the App), operated by Ivan Lim, handles information when connected to third-party platforms such as TikTok. The App is a private tool used by the account owner to automate their own content. 2. Information We Access. When you connect a TikTok account, the App may access basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) via the user.info.basic scope, public account statistics via the user.info.stats scope, and the ability to upload and publish videos via the video.upload and video.publish scopes. The App only accesses data needed to perform actions you explicitly authorize. 3. How We Use Information. Accessed information is used solely to authenticate your connected account and to upload and publish content that you create or provide. We do not sell, rent, or share your information for marketing. 4. Data Storage and Retention. Authentication tokens are stored securely within the account owner's own automation environment (n8n) and used only to perform authorized actions. Tokens can be revoked at any time from your TikTok account settings. 5. Data Sharing. We do not share accessed data with any third party except the platform the data originates from, such as sending your video to TikTok to publish it as you instructed. 6. Security. We take reasonable measures to protect stored credentials. However, no method of storage is completely secure. 7. Your Rights. You may disconnect the App and revoke access at any time through your TikTok account Security and login settings, and may request deletion of stored authorization data by contacting us. 8. Children's Privacy. The App is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors. 9. Changes. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. 10.
